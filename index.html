    Neurobagel

    An open ecosystem for participant data discovery across decentralized neuroscience datasets

    Intuitive tools for harmonizing your neuroscience datasets using community standards and making them discoverable to external researchers, without uploading or sharing raw data.

    Get started
    Stay updated

    Local control, global discovery

    Share your datasets without uploading them to a central location. Your raw data stays local and secure, and you control exactly what information researchers can discover about your data.

    Find participant data for your next study

    Run federated searches across globally distributed datasets to discover participants matching your cohort inclusion criteria. Then learn how to access the data no matter where it lives, all in one place.

    Make datasets interoperable through shared vocabularies

    Make your data ready for sharing by standardizing metadata and variable names with FAIR vocabularies and community standards through an intuitive graphical interface.

    Connect to a global data network

    Connect your datasets to a growing international community of data providers using Neurobagel, and make your data more visible to researchers worldwide.

    Create a secure data catalog across sites

    Quickly learn about and explore data availability across sites in your consortium, even before full data transfer is possible.

    Need to create a data dictionary for messy tabular data?

    Describe variables and values in your data tables using a harmonized data model, completely locally and in the browser.

    Looking for data for your study?

    Run a federated query to create your own cross-dataset participant cohort based on clinical-demographic and imaging parameters.
